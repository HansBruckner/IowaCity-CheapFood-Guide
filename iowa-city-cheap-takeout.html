Cheap Takeout in Iowa City

Need a cheap meal you can eat in your room, at the library, or on the walk home? This page lists the best cheap takeout in Iowa City, from $1.75 street tacos to a $9 burrito bowl. Every restaurant here offers carry out, and each listing shows the price, the distance from campus, and the best day to order.

Prices are for one person before tax and tip. Distances are walking miles from the Pentacrest, so you can tell at a glance whether a pickup fits between classes.

How to save money on carry out

Pick up instead of paying for delivery

Delivery apps often add service and delivery fees on top of the menu price. Walking to pick up your own order keeps the price on the menu as the price you pay.

Call ahead

Phoning in your order before you leave means your food is ready when you arrive, which saves time between classes.

Go at off-peak times

Lunch combos and early evening specials often cost less than dinner, and the pickup line is shorter.

Plan for leftovers

Rice bowls and burrito bowls are big enough to make two meals, so one takeout order can cover lunch and dinner.

A takeout week for about $17

Here is one way to eat carry out three times for under $20 before tax and tip:

  • Tuesday: three tacos from Dubuque Street Taqueria, $5.25
  • Thursday night: two cheese slices from Gilbert Street Slice Shop, $4.00
  • Friday: a two-item bao box from Bowl and Bao Express, $8.00

The total is $5.25 + $4.00 + $8.00 = $17.25.
