Eat well in Iowa City without emptying your wallet

Penny Plate IC is a directory of cheap meals around Iowa City. Whether you have ten minutes between classes, a Friday night with friends, or a week of groceries you can't afford to skip, we list where the good deals are, how far each place is from campus, and which day of the week to go.

Every listing shows the price, the deal, the distance from the Pentacrest, and whether you can dine in, carry out, or both. Browse by price, plan your week, or look up what your student ID can get you.

Student picks

Our student reviewers visit each place at least twice before it is listed. Here is what they keep coming back to.

How we find deals

Our team checks menus and posted specials in person, confirms prices at the register, and re-checks every listing each month. A deal only makes the directory if a student can actually get it without a coupon hunt. If a restaurant changes its offer, we update the listing and note the date we last verified it.
