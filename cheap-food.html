Tips for eating cheap in Iowa City

Go at off-peak times

Lunch combos and early evening specials often cost less than dinner, and the lines are shorter.

Split and save

Burger baskets and pasta plates are big. Splitting one with a friend can bring a meal under $5 each.

Ask about the drink

A free or discounted drink can be worth $2 or more. Several listings include one with a student ID, so check the student discounts page.
