Iowa City Student Food Discounts

Your student ID is worth more than you might think. Many restaurants near campus offer discounts, free drinks, or special offers just for students, and most of them are never advertised on the menu. This page collects the best student food discounts in Iowa City, plus loyalty cards, first-order offers, and group deals that stack with them.

Always show your student ID before you order, since most cashiers cannot add a discount after the receipt is printed.

Special offers

Birthday freebie

Sunny Side Bean and Rice Cafe

Free burrito bowl on your birthday week with a photo ID. Bring a friend and they pay only for their own bowl.

Punch card

Dubuque Street Taqueria

Buy nine tacos at regular price and the tenth is free. Ask for a punch card at the register and keep it in your phone case.

First order offer

Gilbert Street Slice Shop

Your first carry-out order includes a free garlic knot. Use it at lunch, when the shop is quieter and the slices are fresh out of the oven.

Study group bundle

Clinton Street Burger Barn

Groups of four or more get a free shared basket of onion rings with their order on Wednesdays.

Early bird

Coralville Strip Bagel Cart

Buy a bagel breakfast sandwich before 10 a.m. and add a small coffee for $1.

Welcome week deal

Iowa Avenue Supper Club

During the first week of each semester, the prix fixe is $16.99 for anyone with a student ID.

Student tips

Carry your ID everywhere

A student ID in your phone case means you never miss a discount when plans change on the way home.

Ask what is not on the menu

Some of the best deals, like free drinks and dessert bites, are only mentioned if you ask. A friendly question costs nothing.

Go with friends

Group bundles and shared baskets turn a $10 meal into a $6 meal per person when you split them.
