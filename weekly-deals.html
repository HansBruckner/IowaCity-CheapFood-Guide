Iowa City Food Deals, Week by Week

The cheapest meal in town changes with the day of the week. This page lays out the best weekly food deals in Iowa City from Monday through Sunday, so you can plan your meals and your budget ahead of time. Each deal lists the price, the distance from campus, and whether it works for dining in, carry out, or both.

Deals are checked at the start of every month. If a special has changed, the listing shows the date we last confirmed it.

Day-by-day details

Monday

$14 to $15.50

Start the week with a sit-down meal. Riverside Ramen Bar offers a large bowl for $14 with a free soft-boiled egg, and Ped Mall Pasta Kitchen sells a pasta, salad, and garlic bread combo for $15.50. Both are dine in friendly, and the ramen bar also does carry out.

Tuesday

$1.75 per taco

Taco Tuesday at Dubuque Street Taqueria is the cheapest filling meal of the week. Three tacos and a drink still come in under $8. Order at the counter and take it to go if the dining room is packed.

Wednesday

$9.25

Clinton Street Burger Barn serves a burger basket with fries for $9.25, and the booths are big enough for a study group. Dine in only on Wednesdays.

Thursday

$2 slices

After 9 p.m., Gilbert Street Slice Shop drops cheese slices to $2. It is carry out only and gets busy once the library closes, so arrive by 9:15 for a short line.

Friday

$8

Bowl and Bao Express serves a two-item bao box for $8 all day, on top of its weekday $7 rice bowl combo from 11 a.m. to 2 p.m. Dine in or carry out.

Saturday

$3.99

The Coralville Strip Bagel Cart is a short drive or bus ride from campus. The $3.99 breakfast sandwich is available until 10 a.m., which makes it a good stop before a weekend errand run.

Sunday

$9 to $18.99

Two good options: Sunny Side Bean and Rice Cafe has a $9 burrito bowl with unlimited chips, and Iowa Avenue Supper Club offers a two-course prix fixe for $18.99. Both are great for a relaxed end to the weekend.

Pick your best day

Cheapest day overall

Tuesday. Tacos at $1.75 each are hard to beat for the price.

Best day for a group

Wednesday or Sunday. Both have roomy dine-in options and shareable portions.

Best day for late night

Thursday. The $2 slice deal starts at 9 p.m. and runs while the shop is open.

Plan a $40 week

Here is one way to eat out four times in a week for about $40 before tax and tip: tacos on Tuesday ($5.25 for three), a burger basket on Wednesday ($9.25), a bao box on Friday ($8), and a Sunday burrito bowl ($9). That leaves room for a $2 slice on Thursday night and a bagel sandwich on Saturday ($3.99), and the total still lands near $38.
